We welcome original research, systematic reviews, and case studies from scholars and practitioners worldwide, with particular support for early-career researchers.

About JMEHS

About the Journal

Intro

Journal of Medicine, Exercise and Health Science is an English-language, open-access academic journal based in Canada. The journal is positioned as a comprehensive health science journal with medicine and health research as its central focus, and with distinctive coverage of exercise science, sports medicine, rehabilitation, sports nutrition, physical activity, and population health.

The journal aims to publish high-quality original research and scholarly reviews in medicine, exercise science, rehabilitation science, nutrition, public health, health promotion, and related interdisciplinary fields. It seeks to promote academic communication between medical research, exercise-based interventions, rehabilitation practice, nutritional support, physical activity promotion, and population health management. The journal also encourages research that contributes to the translation of scientific evidence into clinical practice, exercise training, rehabilitation guidance, and public health services.

The journal accepts manuscripts written in English. All submissions should present a clear scientific question, appropriate study design, reliable data, rigorous statistical analysis, clear academic writing, and adequate ethical documentation.

Mission
Our Purpose

The Journal of Medicine, Exercise and Health Science was founded in 2025 in Canada with a mission to provide an accessible, rigorous venue for kinesiology and movement science research from scholars around the world.

We are committed to open-access publishing so that research findings are freely available to practitioners, students, and the public, advancing the field beyond institutional barriers.

Aims and Scope

The journal is a comprehensive medical and health science journal with a special focus on exercise, rehabilitation, nutrition, and public health. Priority will be given to studies with strong methodological quality, clear practical value, and potential clinical, health, or translational significance.

Research Areas
  • Clinical medicine and health research
  • Sports medicine and exercise rehabilitation
  • Exercise training and athletic performance
  • Sports nutrition and metabolic health
  • Physical activity and chronic disease prevention
  • Public health and population health
  • Health in older adults, adolescents, and special populations
  • Medical health, healthcare, and health promotion
  • Sports injury prevention, functional assessment, and rehabilitation intervention
  • Interdisciplinary research involving medicine, exercise, nutrition, rehabilitation, and public health

Manuscripts from the above fields may be published in the same issue. The journal does not publish separate discipline-specific issues by default. Instead, it is organized under a broad health science framework and encourages interdisciplinary integration among medicine, exercise science, rehabilitation, nutrition, and population health.

Peer Review Policy
Review Process

All submissions undergo double-blind peer review by at least two independent reviewers with relevant expertise. Authors and reviewers remain anonymous throughout the process.

The typical review period is 4–8 weeks from submission. Authors receive detailed reviewer feedback regardless of the editorial decision.

Author Guidelines

Submit a Manuscript

Before You Submit
Article Types
Original Research

Empirical studies with clear methodology, results, and discussion. 4,000–8,000 words.

Systematic Review

Comprehensive literature reviews with clear inclusion criteria. 5,000–10,000 words.

Narrative Review

Thematic synthesis of existing research. Suitable for early-career researchers. 4,000–7,000 words.

Case Study

Detailed analysis of a specific case with clinical or scientific relevance. 2,000–4,000 words.

Formatting
Manuscript Requirements
  • File format: Microsoft Word (.docx) or LaTeX
  • Language: English (British or American, consistent throughout)
  • Font: Times New Roman or similar serif, 12pt, double-spaced
  • Citation style: APA 7th edition
  • Abstract: 200–250 words with 4–6 keywords
  • Figures and tables: embedded in manuscript, also provided separately
  • Blind submission: remove all identifying author information from manuscript file
Process
Review Timeline
Week 1 Initial editorial screening for scope and formatting
Week 2–6 Double-blind peer review by 2 independent reviewers
Week 7–8 Editorial decision and reviewer feedback returned to author
After rev. Revision review, copyediting, and online-first publication
