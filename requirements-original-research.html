Article Type · Requirements

Original Research Articles

← Back to Submit
Overview

Original Research Articles should report research with a clear objective, complete study design, appropriate data analysis, and reliable conclusions.

Structure
Required Sections
  • Introduction and Objective
  • Materials and Methods
  • Results
  • Discussion
  • Conclusions
  • References

Subheadings may be used where appropriate, but the basic structure above should be maintained.

Abstract
Structured Abstract

Must contain 1,400–1,800 characters (including spaces), divided into:

  • Introduction and Objective
  • Materials and Methods
  • Results
  • Conclusions
Limits
Length, References, Tables & Figures
  • Main text: up to 20,000 characters (excluding abstract and references)
  • References: up to 25
  • Tables and figures: up to 8 total, each self-explanatory with clear titles and legends
Materials and Methods
Methodological Requirements
  • Describe study population, design, sample source, inclusion/exclusion criteria, and measurement instruments in sufficient detail
  • Human studies: state ethics committee name, approval number, and informed consent method
  • Clinical trials: provide registry name and registration number
  • Statistical analysis: state software, version, significance level, tests used, effect sizes, and confidence intervals
